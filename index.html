/ About

Ten years. One cloud.
Infinite possibilities.

In 2016, IBM made quantum computing accessible to anyone on the planet with an internet connection. That was the beginning of a decade-long experiment in democratizing one of the most profound technologies ever conceived.

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 gathers students, researchers, engineers, and curious minds to reflect on that journey — and to explore what comes next. Through talks, workshops, panels, and hands-on sessions, we'll examine the ideas that have shaped quantum computing and the ones that will define its future.

No quantum physics degree required. Just curiosity.

Superposition

While unobserved, a qubit holds both 0 and 1 simultaneously with quantum probabilities. Measurement forces it to choose.

Entanglement

Two qubits can be linked such that measuring one instantly determines the state of the other, regardless of distance.

Interference

Quantum algorithms use interference to amplify correct answers and cancel wrong ones — the engine of quantum speedup.
